# Mathly

Mathly is a client-side, mobile-first daily math habit for older teenagers and adults who want to get faster and more reliable at everyday school algebra. The UI uses Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, and the original shadcn/ui components (Radix base, `radix-vega` style, neutral base color) generated with the shadcn CLI. Progress remains local; there is no account, backend, or keyboard input. The design goal is voluntary daily practice with a friction budget of one tap per day ("Los"): by default the app decides (mixed practice), and the learner may optionally focus on one topic with a single tap; there is no choice of level, duration or settings. The tone is calm and factual (du-address, muted colours); there are no XP, levels, stars or boss tasks, because extrinsic rewards can crowd out interest (overjustification effect). Progress is the **Rechen-Level** and its curve. See `plan.md` for the full concept (section 1a documents this revision) and the ideas that are not built yet.

## Learning flow

- **Home screen**: a large ring shows the Gesamt-Level (sum of all five category levels, 500 = full circle) with the change of the last run; the centre ring is the button for mixed practice and each of the five topic rings around it is a button that starts a run focused on that topic. There is no separate start button. The topic with the lowest level (`recommendedTopic` in `session.js`, ties at random) is highlighted as "Empfohlen" once something was practised; before the first run the centre ring is the recommendation. A one-line hint shows until the first run. Landscape uses two columns: text left, rings right at full height. Only the recommended topic ring pulses (the centre ring while nothing is recommended): an outer halo behind the ring animates (`animate-halo`), never the ring or its text, so numbers stay sharp. The last run's change is drawn on each ring like the level bar in the run (grey/dark = previous level, green = gained, red = lost); there are no +/- numbers. All six rings (centre and topics) have the same size, number and label style and the same ring colour; the recommendation is shown only by the halo and the "Empfohlen" badge.
- **Header**: every screen uses the same opaque, fixed-height `AppHeader` (no blur or transparency, so iOS glass chrome stays crisp) with three columns: the screen's own item on the left (Home and the badge celebration "Mathly", Badges its title, runs the close button), today's correct/answered count and practice time (`DailyStats`: ✓ 24/28 ⏱ 03:20, live during runs) exactly centred, and the theme toggle on the right.
- **Run**: open-ended, there are no daily/extra rounds and no fixed length. Mixed runs interleave all categories; focused runs draw only the chosen one. The header shows the close button, today's stats and the theme toggle; below it a level bar for the current category (grey = level at run start, green grows to the right with gained levels, red is taken off to the left with lost levels; there is no separate +/- number); the line between task and answers reads "Gleichungen Lv 8 🔥 3 · Wähle das richtige Ergebnis" (category, level, streak, instruction), and after a wrong answer only the explanation; every 10 tasks give a short vibration, no stop. Tapping another ring ends the current run and starts a new one.
- **Summary**: closing a run with at least one task shows level change, accuracy and improved topics on the home screen and marks the day as practised.
- **Five micro-task families** (every task is exactly one step, interleaved in mixed runs; the family is never shown to the learner and only steers task selection internally): fractions (simplify, add, multiply, divide, percent), terms (collect, expand from level 3), powers and roots (including negative and fractional exponents), linear equations (one operation: move a constant or divide; integers first, fractions later), and squares/roots/binomial formulas. Quadratic equations were removed on purpose: the building blocks (binomials, roots, terms) are practised until they are automatic first. Learners select the right result from answer tiles. A correct answer advances automatically with no full-screen flash. A wrong choice keeps the line visible (correct tile green, chosen tile red) together with a one-sentence explanation of the rule behind the step; the "Weiter" button in the footer advances; the screen itself is never tappable.
- Rational arithmetic is exact. Fractions are reduced and whole numbers are rendered without a denominator.
- Tasks, intermediate lines, and answer choices are rendered as TeX using KaTeX and its local fonts, including offline use.
- The app never scrolls (not even rubber-banding) on any view or viewport (only the badge list scrolls inside its own area), including an iPhone SE (375×667, Safari ≈ 553 pt visible). The shell is a fluid `100dvh` layout; task card and answer grid share the remaining height in proportion. Landscape (`max-height: 500px`) uses two columns. Pinch zoom stays available; double-tap zoom, text selection and tap highlights are disabled.
- Navigation is a bottom tab bar with two destinations (Heute, Badges), hidden during a run and on the summary.
- Feedback uses several channels: ✓/✗ icon, colour, shake on a wrong tile, vibration where available.
- **Badges** (replaces the former statistics view): a scrollable list of badge groups, each a grid (3 columns portrait, 6 landscape; every group has exactly six badges) of `MilestoneBadge` cards from Trophy UI (`src/components/ui/milestone-badge.tsx`/`milestone-grid.tsx`, MIT, adapted). Earned badges are a coloured medal (topic colour or group colour) with a white lucide icon; open ones are grey with a ring showing the progress. Names are short and read together with the group title (e.g. "Tage-Streak" → "7 Tage"). Groups and targets live in `badges.js`: level per topic (5/10/25/50/75/100), total level (25/50/100/250/400/500), tasks solved (10/50/100/500/1000/5000), practice days (1/3/7/14/30/100), practice time (15/30 min, 1/2/5/10 h), Tage-Streak (3/5/7/14/30/60 days in a row), Aufgaben-Streak (5/10/15/25/50/100 correct in a row) and all-rounder (all topics Lv 5/10/25/50/75/100). Unseen badges that no longer exist after a catalogue change are dropped on load.
- The UI language follows the browser (German or English; no switcher). All visible text and ARIA labels live in `src/lib/i18n.ts` (identical keys in both languages, enforced by tests). The PWA manifest is language-neutral.
- Light and dark themes follow the system setting and can be switched in every header (`mathfeed-theme`).
- Formulas use fixed base font sizes (40 px for tasks, 21 px for answers) and an independent inner transform to fit the available space. All answers within a step share one scale; the task scales independently. Formulas become visible after fonts have loaded.

## Adaptive model

- Each family is a skill with ten difficulty levels (`profileFor` and the per-family generators in `math-engine.js` control number ranges, step count and which rules appear). `generateTask(type, level)`.
- Per skill, `adaptive.js` runs an immediate staircase: every skill has an integer level 1–100 (start 0; level 0 still draws the easiest tasks). A task without any wrong answer gives +1, a task with an error −1, applied at once; the next task of that skill is drawn from the new level (also the repeat of a failed task). No phases, streaks or placement tests.
- **Rechen-Level** (`overallLevel`/`topicLevel` in `skills.js`): each of the five categories has a level 1–100 (`rewards.topicLevels`, one curve point per run for every topic, so curves stay aligned, `rewards.topicHistory`, last 90); task generators in `math-engine.js` scale from level 1 to 100 (every ten levels unlock a new tier of task kinds, numbers keep growing in between). The headline level is the plain sum of all category levels (0–500), so every +1/−1 is directly verifiable. The first run also stores the start levels as a baseline point (`recordBaseline`), so curves and ±numbers appear after the first run. `recordLevel` stores it after each finished run (main or bonus, last 90 values) and follows the real level including setbacks. Home shows the change of the last run on the total and each category ring (`buildChart`/`levelChange` in `level-series.js`).
- Spacing in days (`retention.js`): stability doubles per successful day and halves after an error; retention `R = e^(−days/S)`; a skill below 0.8 counts as faded and gets priority in the run planner (`session.js`) together with weakness, novelty and noise, without the same family three times in a row.
- No freezes or weekly goals. `completeDay` in `rewards.js` records the last practised day, the run count and the day streak (consecutive practised days, a gap restarts it at 1); the day streak only feeds the badges.

## Step-rule engine

Generators create structured exercise state. Fixed-line families (fractions, terms, squares) store the whole chain of equivalent lines and the wrong alternatives per step; linear equations and power/root tasks use rules that apply one transformation or exact calculation and generate misconception distractors. Equation rules preserve the solution set; term rules preserve equivalence; power and root tasks use positive bases and exact real roots. `explanationKey(task)` maps each step to a rule explanation in `i18n.ts`. Every task is a single step. Choices are tappable tiles; no text field or native keyboard is used.

`mathgenerator` is a Python problem/solution generator, not a browser-side step solver. It may inform exercise templates, but the client-side rule catalog owns the pedagogical next-step logic.

## Progress and storage

State v6 in `localStorage` (`mathfeed-state-v6`): `{ version, skills, rewards, session, daily }`; `rewards` holds the last practised day, the run count, the Rechen-Level and its history, per-topic levels and histories, and for the badges the day streak, the best correct streak, the lifetime practice seconds and `badges` (badge id → day earned). Badges are awarded by `awardBadges` on every save and once on load (so badges reachable from saved data appear at once); earned stays earned. Older saves start the lifetime time with the seconds of the retained days. Unknown or outdated fields (old XP, stars, streak data, quadratic skills) are dropped when loading. The v1–v5 keys are migrated by keeping only the daily statistics. Practice time accrues only while a run is open, the tab is visible and focused; it is shown live in the run header and on the home screen. Daily totals use the `Europe/Berlin` calendar date. Statistics retain a rolling seven-day window.

## Development

- `npm run dev` starts the Vite development server.
- `npm test` runs the domain, learning-model and UI layout tests.
- `npm run build` type-checks and builds the offline-ready PWA into `dist/`.
- Add further UI components with `npx shadcn@latest add <component>`; they are written to `src/components/ui/` (configuration in `components.json`). Behind a TLS-intercepting proxy, run the CLI with `NODE_OPTIONS=--use-openssl-ca`.
- Layout: `src/App.tsx` (shell and mode switch), `src/components/` (home, run, summary, badges, bottom nav, task types, math block, theme), `src/hooks/` (run flow, active timer, formula fitting), `src/lib/` (state, i18n, `cn`). The domain modules (`math-engine.js`, `adaptive.js`, `skills.js`, `retention.js`, `session.js`, `rewards.js`, `badges.js`, `dates.js`, `rng.js`, `stats-data.js`, `format.js`, `math-layout.js`) stay plain JavaScript in the repository root.

## Streak-Bonus und Motivation
- Pro Kategorie zählt eine Serie fehlerfreier Aufgaben (persistiert, Fehler setzt auf 0). Sie läuft weiter; bei jedem Vielfachen von 5 (5, 10, 15 …) gibt es +5 Level (Deckel 100). Anzeige als Flamme mit der weiterlaufenden Zahl im Aufgaben-Header (auch beim Bonus); der Bonus erscheint im Konfetti als „Lv +5“. Der Levelsprung der Aufgabe (+1/−1/+5) steht live neben dem Level im Header.
- Vor der täglichen Hauptrunde zeigt der Home-Screen einen Motivationstext mit dem Gesamt-Level.
- **Belobigungen (Party-Screens)**: Streak-Bonus und jedes neu erreichte Badge bekommen eine eigene Einblendung mit Konfetti (`src/components/celebration.tsx`). Passiert beides bei einer Aufgabe, kommt erst der Streak-Bonus, dann per „Weiter“ jedes Badge einzeln, danach die nächste Aufgabe. Nach einer falschen Antwort steht zuerst die Erklärung, die Belobigungen folgen nach „Weiter“. Neue Badges stehen in `rewards.unseenBadges`, bis sie gezeigt wurden: Wer die Runde mitten in der Warteschlange über X verlässt (das gerade gezeigte Badge gilt als gesehen), wer die App schließt oder wer beim Rundenende ein Badge erreicht (z. B. Übungstage), sieht die restlichen Badges auf dem Home-Bildschirm einzeln, nur mit „Weiter“ (Header mit Theme-Schalter) und ohne Navigation. Badges, die beim ersten Laden rückwirkend aus alten Spielständen vergeben werden, werden still vergeben.
- Nur der empfohlene Kreis (sonst die Mitte) hat einen sanft pulsierenden äußeren Halo (`animate-halo`, bei reduzierter Bewegung aus); Kreis und Text skalieren nicht.

- Der Bildschirm selbst ist nie klickbar: nach einem Fehler und im Konfetti geht es nur über den „Weiter“-Button im Footer weiter (Platz bleibt reserviert, damit nichts springt).
